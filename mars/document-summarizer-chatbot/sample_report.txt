Monthly progress report for Block B. The foundation work for Block B was completed on 12 March, four days behind the original schedule. The delay was caused by late delivery of reinforcement steel from the supplier and two days of heavy rain. Concrete pouring for the ground floor slab started on 15 March and is currently 60 percent complete. The crew size was increased from 18 to 24 workers to recover lost time.
Material status is mostly on track. Cement stock is sufficient for the next three weeks. Steel deliveries have been rescheduled to weekly instead of fortnightly to avoid another shortage. Sand and aggregate are being sourced from a second vendor as backup.
Safety observations this month include two near-miss incidents related to scaffolding. Both were reviewed and additional guard rails were installed. All workers completed the monthly safety briefing. Helmet compliance was recorded at 97 percent during site inspections.
The revised completion date for the ground floor slab is 2 April. The project team expects to recover the remaining delay by the end of April if material deliveries stay on schedule. Budget utilisation stands at 41 percent against 38 percent planned, mainly due to overtime costs.
